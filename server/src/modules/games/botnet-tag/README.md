# Botnet Tag (server)

Server-authoritative infection tag for a class: 3 to 8 players (bots fill to 3), one Patient Zero (two at 7+), 3-minute rounds, patch-station puzzles grant 15 s immunity. Task record: G-TAG in [TASKS.md](../../../../TASKS.md). The pure core is `arena`, `rules`, `puzzles`, `rounds` and `bots`; `socket.js`, `protocol.js` and `ruleset.js` add the network and saving. The client is in [client/src/games/botnet-tag/](../../../../../client/src/games/botnet-tag/README.md).

## Round rules

- **Lobby:** a countdown starts once every student, and any staff who pressed Ready, is ready and there are at least 3 players. Bots fill when someone is ready. A student who has not pressed Ready within 45 s of joining, of their last toggle or of the last round is closed with 4410 (`you.readyBy` carries the deadline). Staff are never removed and never hold anyone up.
- **Watching:** staff who have not pressed Ready when the round starts become watchers: out of the player list, untouchable, not in the results (`watching: true`, `you: null` in their snapshot). They rejoin the lobby when the round ends.
- **Start:** `pickSpawns` keeps the closest survivor as far from Patient Zero as the spawn set allows (22 m with 3 players). For the first 5 s (`graceEndsAt` in the snapshot) every tag is refused and a bot Patient Zero stands still, so survivors have a head start. A human Patient Zero may move.
- **Movement:** each accepted step spends a credit that refills at 1.25 times the speed in force, capped at about 1 s of walking, so 25 messages a second travel no further than one.
- **End:** everyone infected, or the clock. If a leave empties a side, or leaves only a Patient Zero whose botnet never spread, the round ends abandoned (`sideWon: null`, `unsaved: 'abandoned'`). A botnet that had really spread still wins when the last clean player leaves.
- **Races:** a right answer at a station someone else has just patched sets `you.notice` for 4 s, with no lockout.

## Connect

`GET /api/botnet-tag` upgrade, routed by `server.js` to `app.locals.botnetTag`. Same checks as presence (403 bad origin, 401 no session). A student is placed by the server in their active class and into an open lobby round (or a new one, cap 8); a student with no class is closed 4403. Staff send `join {cohortId}` first (instructor needs an active assignment, admin any active class), play flagged `isStaff`, and are never saved. One round per socket; one game socket per account (the newest closes the older with 4409).

Close codes: 4401 session ended (`replaced` after a newer login, SESS-01, or signed out, including at once when an administrator disables or deletes the account), 4403 no class or class changed, 4408 too many bad messages, 4409 superseded, 4410 idle in the lobby, 1009 oversized, 1011 the round failed (a tick that throws closes that round only, and the client reconnects).

## Protocol (JSON, 2304-byte frame cap)

Client to server, with per-second limits: `join {cohortId}` (staff only, 3), `ready {ready}` (5), `move {x,z,yaw}` (25), `sprint {}` (3), `tag {target}` (10), `station {id}` (5), `submit {submission}` (5, at most 2 KB), `leave {}` (3, closes the socket). Unknown, malformed or over-limit messages are dropped and count as strikes (30 in 10 s closes 4408). Moves, tags, sprints and puzzles are validated by the round rules, so a rejected move simply keeps the last accepted position.

Server to client: `snapshot` (`sid` is your per-connection id, plus the round snapshot: phase, players (each with a public `look`: wardrobe item ids only, built by `presence/look.js` the same way presence builds it; bots get the default outfit), stations, `you` with only your own puzzle, and `failReason` during your own lockout) at 15 Hz while playing and at most 2 Hz in the lobby (also at once on join, ready, station and submit), `events` (tags, patches, start, end), `results` (all players' scores, no ids beyond the per-connection sid, plus `unsaved`: `short`, `abandoned` or null), `saved {saved, score?, maxScore?, reason?}` to each student in the round. The snapshot also carries `graceEndsAt` and `watching`, and `you` carries `notice` and `readyBy`. No email, user id or puzzle answer is ever sent. `you.failReason` is one fixed line per puzzle kind for a wrong answer (`FAIL_REASONS` in `puzzles.js`) or a late one (`LATE_REASON`), set on lockout and shown only while `lockedUntil` is in the future; it names the kind of mistake, is independent of the answer and the submission, and is never in other players' snapshots.

## Saving

At round end the service calls `recordCompletedAttempt` once per human non-staff player: class context, mode `practice`, `resultKey` = round id + play number + sid. A lobby keeps one round id across plays, so the play number gives each play its own attempt; attempts saved under the old round id + sid keys stay as they are and replay by that key. A round under 20 s or an abandoned one saves nothing and sends `saved {saved:false, reason}`. Ruleset `botnet-tag@2` is server-recorded and noncompetitive, so it is ranked only as a class practice best score. Its maximum is 930: `scoreFor` at 180 s, 10 patches, 5 tags (a survivor tagged late can catch at most the other survivors of a full round) and the win; 13 patches as a clean survivor reach the same number, and live and saved scores stop there. `botnet-tag@1` stays registered with its maximum of 3520 and no cap, so old attempts keep their score. State is outcome, side won, survival seconds, patches, tags, plus `startedInfected` and `cleanAtEnd` (clean players left, bots included) for the achievements; the score is recomputed from them (survival seconds + 50 per patch + 30 per tag + 100 for the winning side). A player who disconnects before the round ends is not saved. A failed save sends `saved {saved:false}`; there is no retry.

## Limits and tests

One interval per live round, cleared when the round empties; `close()` stops everything. Tests use an injected clock, a parked tick (`tickAll`) and `inspectRounds` (test seams on the service). Browser specs use `POST /api/test/botnet-tag/control` (`testControls.js`: choose Patient Zero, gather players, read the open puzzle's answer, move the round clock on, finish). `server.js` asks for it only when `NODE_ENV` is `test`, `app.js` mounts it only when the process also runs as `test` (a 404 otherwise, covered by a test), and the router refuses to build anywhere else. The same server stands its bots still (`botsIdle`), so nothing moves under a browser test. Unit: `server/tests/unit/botnet-tag`; integration: `server/tests/integration/botnet-tag/socket.test.js`.
