# Botnet Tag (client)

Browser half of `botnet-tag` (G-TAG), served at `/games/botnet-tag` from the "Server Yard" warehouse: east lane of the 3D wharf at (40, 20.5), and a `warehouse` building at (240, 600) in the 2D town. The server rules are in [server/src/modules/games/botnet-tag/README.md](../../../../server/src/modules/games/botnet-tag/README.md).

## Presentation summary

- **What:** infection tag for a class. 3 to 8 players (bots fill to 3), one Patient Zero tags survivors into the botnet, survivors patch stations for 15 s of immunity, 3-minute rounds.
- **Concept taught:** how worms and botnets spread and why patching and filtering stop them (CS440 `10-software-security.pptx` worms, `1-basics.pptx` botnets, `09a-network-security.pptx` firewalls; the patch tasks themselves are a team design).
- **How to play:** open Botnet Tag, press Ready in the lobby; when everyone is ready a 10 s countdown starts. Results show the winning side and your score, with Play again or Leave.
- **3D only:** the catalog entry sets `needs3d`, so the 2D card and the Places row show a "3D" badge. Without WebGL2 the page shows "Needs 3D" and Back to town; the 2D town setting does not block it.
- **Technical points:** `BotnetTagGame.vue` composes `TagLobby`, `TagHud`, `TagResults` and `NeedsThreeD` over `useTagSocket.js`, which speaks the server's JSON protocol on `/api/botnet-tag` (reconnect with backoff, close codes 4401/4403/4408/4409, moves capped at 20 per second). The `tag-arena` element is the empty mount point for the 3D arena. Rules helpers are in `tagRules.js`; styles are scoped to `.botnet-tag-game` in `tag.css`.
- **Saving:** the server saves one practice attempt per human non-staff player at round end; the results screen shows the saved score. No leaderboard.
- **Puzzles (stage 3c):** `puzzles/PuzzlePanel.vue` is the shared dialog (icon and title, time bar from the server deadline, Close, Submit; Escape closes, Enter submits when complete, focus returns on close). One body per kind renders the server's `public` payload and emits `{choices}` (`FirewallBody`, `PatchBody`) or `{index}` (`ProcessBody`, `HashBody`, `SpoofBody`, `AccountBody`, the last three index bodies share `ChoiceList.vue`). `PuzzleResult.vue` shows Patched or the lockout seconds. The protocol has no cancel, so Close only hides the panel until the deadline; the answer is never marked before submit.
- **Audio and How to play (stage 3d-ii):** `audio/useTagSound.js` makes the effects with WebAudio (no files): round horn, ticks in the last 10 s (faster in the last 3), tag, infected, patch, lockout buzz, immunity ending, sprint, win and lose. The context is created on the first gesture, effects follow the shared `sharlock.musicMuted` key and the context closes on unmount. `audio/soundCues.js` maps previous and next state plus new events to cue names (unit-tested); `play('sprint')` is for the arena to call. The round loop is `useGameAudio('botnet-tag')` (`/audio/games/botnet-tag-theme.mp3`, silent while the file is missing); a tense layer for the last 30 s is pending that track. A Sound button sits in the immersive shell. `howToPlay.js` and `components/HowToPlayScene.vue` (animated SVG arena, still frame under reduced motion) feed the shared dialog, which opens on the first visit and from the "How to play" button beside the Menu.
- **Achievements and skins (stage 3d-i):** five server-derived achievements from saved rounds: Untouchable (clean all round), Last One Clean (survivors win with one clean player left), Super-spreader (3+ tags), Firewall (4+ patches), Patient Zero Wins (started infected, botnet won). They unlock a hazmat hood, circuit scarf, antivirus shield and patch-cable bandolier in the profile wardrobe, drawn on the 3D Sharlock and shown to class peers. No client code decides any of it.
- **Staff:** instructors and administrators pick a class first, play flagged as staff and are never saved.
- **Status:** in progress (TASKS.md, G-TAG). Stage 3a is the shell: lobby, results and a HUD skeleton wired to live state; stage 3c adds the puzzle panels; stage 3b-i adds the 3D arena. The 3D arena (which will open a puzzle through `station`), How to play, audio and achievements come next.
- **Arena (stage 3b-i):** `arena/` draws the dockyard in Babylon while a round is on screen (countdown, playing, results; the lobby keeps the plain backdrop). `arenaData.js` is a client copy of the server ARENA, kept equal by a test, so drawn stacks are exactly the collision rects. The scene is built from the hub's textures and toon/glow materials and merged into a few draws; the hub graphics tiers set shadows, post, render scale and frame cap, and the hub fps watch lowers one tier at a time but never leaves 3D (below Low it stays on Low). Reduced motion removes the lamp flicker and ring pulses and uses calm rig idles. The local player is a slim kinematic one (`localPlayer.js`, same keys, sprint and jump modules, server `walkable`), the hub's havok player being tied to the wharf; moves go out through `useTagSocket.move` (20 per second) and a server position more than 1.5 m away eases the player back. Others ease toward snapshots; Low draws static meshes instead of rigs. Name labels are DOM, nudged apart and capped at 48 px from the body. Dev only: `window.__tag.debug` (`players()`, `you()`, `tier()`, `simulateFps()`).
- **Limits:** no tagging or station interaction yet (3b-ii); no sprint or jump buttons or touch joystick (Shift and Space only); remote outfits show the default look until the snapshot carries a public look.
