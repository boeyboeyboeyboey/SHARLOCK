# Botnet Tag (client)

Browser half of `botnet-tag` (G-TAG), served at `/games/botnet-tag` from the "Server Yard" warehouse: east lane of the 3D wharf at (40, 20.5), and a `warehouse` building at (600, 580) in the 2D town. The server rules are in [server/src/modules/games/botnet-tag/README.md](../../../../server/src/modules/games/botnet-tag/README.md).

## Presentation summary

- **What:** infection tag for a class. 3 to 8 players (bots fill to 3), one Patient Zero tags survivors into the botnet, survivors patch stations for 15 s of immunity, 3-minute rounds.
- **Concept taught:** how worms and botnets spread and why patching and filtering stop them (CS440 `10-software-security.pptx` worms, `1-basics.pptx` botnets, `09a-network-security.pptx` firewalls; the patch tasks themselves are a team design).
- **How to play:** open Botnet Tag, press Ready in the lobby within 45 seconds; when every student is ready a 10 s countdown starts. For the first 5 seconds of a round nobody can tag and a "Get ready" notice counts down. Results show the winning side and your score, with Play again or Leave.
- **Leaving and help:** How to play, Sound and Leave game stay in the top bar through the lobby and the round (below 768px they shorten to "?" and "Leave", with the full names kept for screen readers). During a countdown or round, Leave game and the site menu's links ask first ("An unfinished round won't be saved"); from the lobby or results they leave at once.
- **Connection and removal:** while the socket reconnects a "Connection lost, reconnecting" banner shows and Ready, Use and the touch controls are off; a new `sid` after a countdown or round shows "Your round ended while you were away" in the lobby. Close 4410 shows "Removed from the lobby" with Rejoin. On a first visit the socket waits until the guide closes, so reading it does not spend the 45 seconds. Opened from the lobby, the guide restarts the limit with a `ready {ready:false}` when it opens and closes, and every 30 s while open, at most three times (`GUIDE_HOLD_MS`, `GUIDE_HOLDS`), so a guide left open still ends in removal after about 2 minutes 15 seconds. If the 3D yard cannot start the page leaves the round and offers Retry. Close 4401 drops the local session and goes to the login page with its reason, as the hub does.
- **3D only:** the catalog entry sets `needs3d`, so the 2D card and the Places row show a "3D" badge. Without WebGL2 the page shows "Needs 3D" and Back to town; the 2D town setting does not block it.
- **Technical points:** `BotnetTagGame.vue` composes `TagLobby`, `TagHud`, `TagResults` and `NeedsThreeD` over `useTagSocket.js`, which speaks the server's JSON protocol on `/api/botnet-tag` (reconnect with backoff, close codes 4401/4403/4408/4409, moves capped at 20 per second). The `tag-arena` element is the empty mount point for the 3D arena. Rules helpers are in `tagRules.js`; styles are scoped to `.botnet-tag-game` in `tag.css`.
- **Saving:** the server saves one practice attempt per human non-staff player at round end; the results screen shows the saved score out of 930, and your class has a practice board. A round under 20 seconds, or one that lost a whole side when someone left, is not saved, and the results say which.
- **Puzzles (stage 3c):** `puzzles/PuzzlePanel.vue` is the shared dialog (icon and title, time bar from the server deadline, Close, Submit; Escape closes, Enter submits when complete, focus returns on close). One body per kind renders the server's `public` payload and emits `{choices}` (`FirewallBody`, `PatchBody`) or `{index}` (`ProcessBody`, `HashBody`, `SpoofBody`, `AccountBody`, the last three index bodies share `ChoiceList.vue`). `PuzzleResult.vue` shows Patched, "Someone patched this station first" (`you.notice`, no lockout) when another player got there first, or the lockout seconds with the server's one-line reason (`you.failReason`: a hint about the kind of mistake for that puzzle, or "Too late"; never the answer). The dialog is modal: Escape works wherever focus is, unless another dialog is open, and Tab stays inside. The protocol has no cancel, so Close only hides the panel until the deadline; the answer is never marked before submit.
- **Audio and How to play (stage 3d-ii):** `audio/useTagSound.js` makes the effects with WebAudio (no files): round horn, ticks in the last 10 s (faster in the last 3), tag, infected, patch, lockout buzz, immunity ending, sprint, win and lose. The context is created on the first gesture, effects follow the shared `sharlock.musicMuted` key and the context closes on unmount. `audio/soundCues.js` maps previous and next state plus new events to cue names (unit-tested); `play('sprint')` is for the arena to call. The round music is `audio/useRoundMusic.js` (AUDIO-05): two `useGameAudio` players, `botnet-tag-theme.mp3` and, for the last 30 s of play, `botnet-tag-final-theme.mp3`; only one plays at a time, the switch is an instant cut, and a missing final file falls back to the round loop for the rest of the visit. A Sound button sits in the immersive shell. `howToPlay.js` and `components/HowToPlayScene.vue` (animated SVG arena, still frame under reduced motion) feed the shared dialog (it also shows the game's catalog difficulty and background knowledge), which opens on the first visit and from the "How to play" button in the top bar.
- **Achievements and skins (stage 3d-i):** five server-derived achievements from saved rounds: Untouchable (clean all round), Last One Clean (survivors win with one clean player left), Super-spreader (3+ tags), Firewall (4+ patches), Patient Zero Wins (started infected, botnet won). They unlock a hazmat hood, circuit scarf, antivirus shield and patch-cable bandolier in the profile wardrobe, drawn on the 3D Sharlock and shown to class peers. No client code decides any of it.
- **Staff:** instructors and administrators pick a class first (joined directly when they have only one; every page of classes is listed), and see a message with Retry or Back to town when there are none or they fail to load. They watch unless they press Ready: a watching player is labelled Watching, has no controls, is not in the round and never holds students up. Staff who play are flagged and never saved.
- **Status:** see the G-TAG record in [TASKS.md](../../../../TASKS.md).
- **Arena (stage 3b-i):** `arena/` draws the dockyard in Babylon while a round is on screen (countdown, playing, results; the lobby keeps the plain backdrop). `arenaData.js` is a client copy of the server ARENA, kept equal by a test, so drawn stacks are exactly the collision rects. The scene is built from the hub's textures and toon/glow materials and merged into a few draws; the hub graphics tiers set shadows, post, render scale and frame cap, and the hub fps watch lowers one tier at a time but never leaves 3D (below Low it stays on Low). A tier change rebuilds the scene, and held movement keys, the joystick and `walkTo` carry over to the new local player, so the player keeps moving. Reduced motion removes the lamp flicker and ring pulses and uses calm rig idles. The local player is a slim kinematic one (`localPlayer.js`, same keys, sprint and jump modules, server `walkable`), the hub's havok player being tied to the wharf; moves go out through `useTagSocket.move` (20 per second) and a server position more than 1.5 m away eases the player back. The first playing frame of a round snaps the local player to the server spawn instead, because the lobby position sits inside the centre stack, where easing never gets out. Others ease toward snapshots; Low draws static meshes instead of rigs. Name labels are DOM, nudged apart and capped at 48 px from the body. Dev only: `window.__tag.debug` (`players()`, `you()`, `tier()`, `simulateFps()`).
- **Interaction (stage 3b-ii):** an infected player tags by touching: `BotnetTagGame.vue` checks snapshot positions every 120 ms and sends `tag {target}` for the nearest clean, non-immune player within 1.7 m (server reach is 2.0 m), at most one every 350 ms; the "Tagged!" toast comes only from the server `tag` event and fades after 3 s. A clean player within 1.8 m of a ready station sees `<kbd>E</kbd> Use` (`UsePrompt.vue`, also a tap target); E, Enter or a tap sends `station {id}` and the puzzle opens from the snapshot; the prompt is hidden while infected, cooling or a puzzle is open, and shows "Locked Ns" during a lockout. On coarse pointers `TouchControls.vue` adds a joystick (`arena.setJoystick`), Jump and Sprint (ring follows this tab's own sprint; `sprint` sound cue plays when a sprint starts), all 44 px or larger. Remote players wear the public `look` from the snapshot; bots wear the default outfit. Dev only: `window.__tag.debug.walkTo(x, z)` walks the local player through the normal movement rules (used by the E2E). The infected player's page sends no tag during the grace period. Below 576px the results table folds Patches, Tags and Win into a line under the name, so it never scrolls sideways.
- **Tests:** `tests/e2e/botnet-tag.spec.js` plays both sides to a saved result through the test-only route of the [server README](../../../../server/src/modules/games/botnet-tag/README.md#limits-and-tests), and a shared lobby; component, socket and arena tests sit in `__tests__/`.
- **Limits:** the server cannot tell a guide's `ready` from any other, so a scripted client could keep restarting its own limit; the touch sprint ring is timed from this tab's own start, so it can differ from the server cooldown after a reconnect; real-device touch and all graphics tiers are not yet checked.
